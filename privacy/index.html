Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026
App: knock-on (com.tadhg.knockon)

This page summarises how knock-on handles data. It is provided for transparency and Google Play requirements; it is not legal advice.

Summary

knock-on is a rugby scores / fixtures companion aimed at Irish provincial fans. Today it works largely from on-device data and optional deep links to third-party score sites. There is no user account yet.

Data we store on your device

  • Followed / favourite teams: saved locally with AsyncStorage (or equivalent on-device storage). This data stays on your device unless you clear app storage or uninstall.
  • We do not currently sync favourites to a server.

Accounts

  • No account / sign-in in the current MVP. We do not collect email, password, or profile data for login.

Push notifications (future)

  • The app may later use Expo push notifications. That would involve an Expo push token (and related device identifiers needed for FCM/APNs) sent to a backend we control so we can deliver alerts you opt into.
  • Until that feature ships, we do not register for push or upload push tokens.
  • When enabled, this policy will be updated.

Third-party score sources

  • In-app live score buttons may deep-link to third-party sites (e.g. Flashscore, Sofascore). Those services have their own privacy policies; we do not scrape their pages for production scores.
  • Optional offline score JSON may be generated with developer tools and third-party APIs using keys that are never shipped inside the app binary. End users of the store build are not sending those API keys.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

  • No third-party ads and no analytics SDKs in the current MVP. If that changes, this section will list providers and purposes.

Children

  • The app is a general sports utility. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Google Play IARC content rating is to be confirmed in Play Console.

Your choices

  • Change followed teams in Settings; clear app data or uninstall to remove local favourites.
  • Once push notifications exist, you can revoke notification permission in system settings.

Changes

  • We will update this page when behaviour changes (accounts, push, analytics, etc.). The in-app Privacy link points at this hosted URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options on the Google Play listing for knock-on (com.tadhg.knockon). A dedicated privacy email may be added to this page later.

Published for Play Store and in-app Settings. Source markdown is maintained in the private knock-on app repository.
